Promote students (end of semester)

Moves everyone up one class: IV Sem → Alumni, III Sem → IV Sem, II Sem → III Sem, I Sem → II Sem. Login IDs and passwords stay the same.

Before pressing it: export your reports, import this semester's results under Past records, and clear the old attendance below. Press it once per semester.
